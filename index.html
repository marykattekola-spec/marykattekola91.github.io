Summary

Twelve years across data migration, systems integration, and multi-domain application delivery — carrying the rare combination of certified authority in analysis (CBAP), delivery (PMP), and product ownership (PSPO) on the same program. I've spent that time doing the unglamorous work that keeps go-live dates real: reconciling data that looks migrated but isn't, catching requirement gaps before they reach UAT, and running the program discipline that keeps everyone honest about scope.

I lead every engagement personally and stay accountable for the outcome end to end. Across data migration, application development & integrations, testing & QA, and project management, I work with a trusted team of specialists who execute the hands-on work in each area — so it runs as one coordinated practice, not four disconnected freelancers.
